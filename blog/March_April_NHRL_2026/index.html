Chippy NHRL March/April 2026 Recap

Going into NHRL, I was excited to test out the new version of Chippy and destroy some electronics. My goal for this competition was to run Chippy into the ground and leave with it completely destroyed especially since it probably won’t fight again until 2026 due to scheduling conflicts.

Fight 1: Split Decision

My first match was against Gallos12, a veteran Fingertech beater bar that last competed in 2022. Chippy quickly ran into a couple of problems that would plague me for the rest of the competition:

  • Blade kept getting stuck: As I alluded to in the version 3 breakdown, changing the weapon geometry didn’t prevent the blade from getting stuck. The geometry change and switching the battery to 5s allowed it to penetrate deeper into opponents, which probably made the issue worse. I got stuck twice, with the match ending after the bots needed to be unstuck a second time. While I wasn’t penalized directly for getting stuck, it hurt my control points.
  • Gear stripping: Switching to a nylon driving gear created a rough surface that wore away the TPU gear on the arm. During normal operation, the arm worked fine for the whole match. However, when I got stuck in Gallos12 and Till Death and tried to pull the blade out, it stripped the gear instantly. This meant I had to replace the TPU gear after every match.

Despite the problems, I won by judges' decision after disabling Gallos12’s weapon motor and damaging its drive.

Fight 2: Fatal Illusion

Hes a horizontal that uses the repeat beetle hub. I accidentally ordered ½” uhmw instead of ¾” for the horizontal forks but they held up he did get a really good gouge on them (may need to go to ¾ for scarier horizontals) but I hit him and cut his weapon wires then snipped his battery leads and won by K.O. I don’t really like how this config drove it was sticky on the right side for some reason so imma tweak it some more.

Fight 3: Power On

This one was rough, he hit my right side and my right side armor, a fork, and a wheel came off. My plastites pulled through. I was kinda hobbling around for the rest of the fight and it went to JD where I lost. The beater forks worked well if it was an off center hit I would win but head on was 50/50. It also boiled down to a driving skill issue. I also had another failure mode of the compact where the gearbox ejects from the motor.

The mission of running Chippy into the ground was accomplished. I ended NHRL with a 3-1 record, the same as in October. I also ended up going through 2 weapon motors, a weapon ESC, and 5 gears. I wish Chippy went a little farther, but a majority of the bot held up great, and I had an amazing time competing!

Fight 1: Captain Chaos

Captain Chaos is an older version of Sad Octopus that was run on Teams and was driven by Cam’s sister, Nora. I was lowkey terrified, the bot has a lot of gyro and was hard to pin because it was so slithery and kept bouncing off everything. I was able to snipe their weapon motor and shift their drisk. The fight ended with me hitting and getting stuck in their bottom plate and hitting their battery.

Fight 2: Deadline

I started the fight down a drive side (because my ESC desoldered) and deadline had weapon issues the whole fight. He also shut off near the end of it. I went weapon on weapon twice, and the hubmotor was fine. The fight ended with me getting stuck in their tpu top armor, and I lost by JD.

Fight 3: Kyrofang

Kyrofang is a generic 4WD vert. Leading up to this fight, I thought it was all over because of electrical issues of figuring out why my drive did not work, then my bot would not bind, but I was able to fix it, and idk something just clicked this fight where I realized I have nothing to lose, so imma run chippy into the ground. The fight was quick. I cut through his top armor, his uprights, and his battery, but I got stuck in his bottom plate.
